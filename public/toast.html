How it connects

A read-only connection you set up in minutes — with us.

Coursed reads your live order activity through Toast's standard API. There's nothing to install on your terminals and nothing for your servers to learn. Our team walks you through it on a single onboarding call.

You authorize a read-only connection

Using Toast's own API access, you grant Coursed permission to read order data only. We do this together on your onboarding call so you never touch a config file.

Coursed starts reading live orders

As tickets are rung in during service, Coursed reads them on a live cycle and scores each table against your service standard — automatically, with no manual entry.

Your board goes live on a back-of-house screen

Open the board on any tablet or TV on the pass. It refreshes itself all service. That's the whole install.

What Coursed reads

Only what it needs to score service.

  • Open and closed orders for the current business date — table, server, timestamps.
  • Line items and modifiers — so it knows drinks, apps, entrées and dessert, and when each fired.
  • Guest counts and check totals — to score per-guest pace and show spend per cover.
  • Menu, table and employee reference data — to label the board with real names.
What Coursed never does

It reads. It never writes.

  • Never changes a ticket, price, or menu in Toast. The connection is read-only by design.
  • Never touches payments or card data. Coursed doesn't process or store payment information.
  • Adds no workflow for servers. No new buttons, no extra taps, nothing to remember mid-shift.
  • Can be revoked any time. You control the connection from your own Toast account.

Security & data

Security and data FAQ.

Does Coursed change anything in our Toast account?

No. The connection is read-only. Coursed reads order data to build the board and never writes to, changes, or deletes anything in Toast — not tickets, not prices, not the menu.

Do you touch payments or card data?

No. Coursed does not process, transmit, or store payment or cardholder data. It reads order activity (items, timing, guest counts, check totals) — not payment instruments.

How is the connection authorized, and can we revoke it?

You authorize a read-only connection through Toast's standard API access during assisted onboarding, and you can revoke it at any time from your own Toast account. Your API credentials are stored encrypted at rest.

What data do you store, and for how long?

Coursed stores the computed service board and rolled-up reporting so you can look back across shifts. We don't sell your data or share it with third parties. Data-retention and deletion terms are covered in our agreement.

Does this add work for our servers?

None. There's nothing to install on your terminals and no new steps for staff. The board reads what your team already rings in.

Is Coursed affiliated with Toast?

No. Coursed is an independent product that connects to Toast through Toast's standard API. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by Toast, Inc.
