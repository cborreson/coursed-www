The idea

What Coursed is

Coursed is a live, per-table view of your dining room. It reads your POS order data and scores every table in real time against a six-step service ladder built from your own hospitality standard — first round, apps, second round, entrées, third round, dessert.

The point isn't to push checks. It's to make your standard visible while there's still time to act: you can see the table that's slipping and fix it at the table, not at closeout. Good hospitality is good business — the check follows.

Prep

Before you begin

Have these ready and setup goes quickly:

  • Your POS. Coursed reads live order data from your point-of-sale. You'll need admin access, or someone on your team who has it — we'll confirm your specific POS when we set up the connection.
  • A screen for the floor. Any TV, tablet, or spare laptop that can open a web browser and stay on during service. This becomes your service board.
  • Your service standard in mind. Roughly when a table should have its first round, apps, entrées, and so on. Sensible defaults are built in — you tune them in Step 4.
  • About 30 minutes. Most of it is done together with the Coursed team on your first call.

Step one

1Sign up & pick a plan

Coursed starts with a 30-day pilot, not a checkout — you run the full product on your own service data before you ever pay. Apply at getcoursed.com and the Coursed team reaches out to get you set up. No card required to begin.

Once you’re set up, your restaurant gets its own board address — yourname.getcoursed.com. At the end of the 30 days you decide whether to subscribe; see Pricing, billing & help for what it costs.

Multiple locations? Don't sign each one up separately — see Multiple locations so they roll up under one account with a single login.

Step two

2Connect your POS

This is the step the Coursed team does with you, on your onboarding call. We connect Coursed to your POS and authorize it to read your order data — nothing else. Coursed only ever reads orders; it can't change tickets, prices, or anything in your POS.

Once connected, your board starts building automatically. Data flows in on a live cycle throughout service, so you don't touch anything to keep it current.

What we need from you: a few minutes with your POS admin to authorize the connection. We'll walk you through exactly where to click.

Step three

3Put the board on the floor

Open yourname.getcoursed.com on the screen you picked, and log in with the board password we set up together. That's it — the board is live.

  • Pick a density. Toggle between Full, Compact, and Mini depending on how big your screen is and how many tables you run. Mini is the usual choice for a wall-mounted kiosk.
  • Filter the view. Show Open, Trouble, or Closed tables to focus on what needs attention right now.
  • Leave it on. The board refreshes itself. Set the screen to stay awake during service and you can forget about it.
Tip: the login is remembered on that screen, so the kiosk stays signed in between shifts.

Wall display

Kiosk mode — full-screen & always-on

For a tablet mounted on the wall or the pass, lock it to the board so it runs edge-to-edge, never sleeps, and can't be swiped away from during service. Works on any recent iPad or Android tablet.

First, sign the tablet in once. The cleanest way is a floor kiosk link: from a manager login, open Scoring → Floor access → Generate kiosk link, then open that link on the tablet. It signs the screen in as a view-only floor board — no password to leave on the wall, and it can't reach your settings. (A normal board login works too; it's just remembered on that screen.) Set the density to Mini or Compact for a wall.

On an iPad

  • Keep it awake. Settings → Display & Brightness → Auto-Lock → Never, and keep the iPad plugged into power.
  • Open the board. In Safari, go to your board (or paste the floor kiosk link). Optional: tap Share → Add to Home Screen for an app icon you can tap to open.
  • Turn on Guided Access. Settings → Accessibility → Guided Access → On, then set a passcode under Passcode Settings.
  • Lock it to the board. With the board on screen, triple-click the top (or side) button → Guided Access → Start. The iPad is now locked to the board; triple-click and enter the passcode to exit.
Permanent install? For a wall tablet that runs 24/7, a dedicated kiosk-browser app like Kiosk Pro adds auto-reload after a Wi-Fi blip and hides every browser control — worth it for a fixed screen.

On an Android tablet

  • Keep it awake. Settings → Display → Screen timeout → the longest option (or turn on Developer options → Stay awake while charging). Keep it plugged in.
  • Open the board. In Chrome, go to your board (or paste the floor kiosk link). Optional: menu → Add to Home screen.
  • Turn on screen pinning. Settings → Security & privacy → App pinning / Screen pinning → On. Open the board, open the app switcher, and pin it. Unpinning asks for your PIN, so no one can wander off the board.
Permanent install? For an always-on wall display, the free Fully Kiosk Browser app is the most reliable — set your board as its start URL and turn on full-screen, keep-screen-on, auto-reload, and start-on-boot.
Either device: keep it on power, set the brightness for the room, make sure it stays on stable Wi-Fi, and pause automatic OS updates that could reboot the tablet mid-service.

Step four

4Set your service standard

Your board comes with sensible defaults, but the whole value is that it reflects your standard. On the settings page you can tune the six rungs of the ladder — how many drinks, when apps should land, entrée timing, dessert — per shift, since brunch and a Friday dinner aren't the same.

  • The six-star ladder. Each rung is a moment of service. Set the per-guest thresholds so a "perfect table" means what it means in your room.
  • Shift windows. Define your service periods so timing rules apply to the right daypart.
  • Board colors. Group plans can match the board to your brand.

Every Coursed subscription includes a fully customizable ladder and drink weighting — the built-in defaults are just a starting point.

Step five

5Categorize your menu

Coursed needs to know which items are drinks, apps, entrées, and desserts so it scores each rung correctly. Most items sort themselves automatically — you just review and fix the handful that don't.

  • Set a whole menu group at once. Instead of tagging item by item, point a menu group at a category — e.g. "Small Plates & Shareables → Apps" — and every item under it follows.
  • Override individual items where a single dish doesn't fit its group.
  • It sticks. New items you add in your POS inherit their group's rule automatically.
Item categorization and drink weighting — fine-tuning how much individual drinks count toward a rung — are both part of every Coursed subscription.

Step six

6Pace alerts included

Pace watches for tables falling behind — past a course deadline (plus a buffer you set) or sitting longer than your max table time — and flags them red on the board so a manager can step in before the guest notices. It’s built into Coursed, not a separate add-on — it’s on and ready to tune to your service.

If a flag isn't warranted — a table that's lingering happily over coffee — a manager can clear the trouble flag right from the table, and reinstate it later if things change.

Day to day

Reading the board

Here's what your team sees during service. It's meant to be glanceable — no training required beyond this.

★★★
Stars

Each table earns up to six stars as it moves up the service ladder. A table lit gold has hit every rung — your standard, met.

✦
Perfect table

A full-ladder table gets the gold treatment — worth calling out to the team.

▲
Trouble flag (Pace)

A red flag means a table is behind on a course or over its time. Tap it for the fire timeline showing where it slipped. Clear it if it's a false alarm.

⚠
Low-guest flag

A gold ⚠ means more entrées than the guest count suggests — usually the cover count was under-entered, which makes the score too easy. Fix the count, or clear the flag if it's correct.

Tap any table to open its fire timeline — a play-by-play of what was ordered and when. A leaderboard ranks servers by how well their tables track to standard.

Look back

Reports

The live board is for right now; reports are for patterns. Pull a date range to see how consistently your room hit each rung, which shifts run strong, and how servers trend over time.

  • Rung completion. How often tables reached each step of the ladder — met vs. available — so you can see exactly where service tends to drop off.
  • Trend over time. Your average scored out of six, tracked across the range.
  • Server leaderboard. Ranked by tables that met standard. Servers with too few tables to judge fairly are held out so small samples don't skew the board.

Reporting and historical lookback of any past date and shift are included in every Coursed subscription.

More than one room

Multiple locations

If you run several venues, they roll up under a single billing group — one account, one login, each location billed as its own line item. You get a group address, yourgroup.getcoursed.com, with one password for the whole group.

  • One login, every board. Sign in once at your group address and switch between your locations from the board — no separate password per venue.
  • Each location keeps its own standard. Scoring, shifts, and menu categories are set per location; the group just ties them together.
  • Cross-location reporting. Compare venues side by side from one place.
Tell the Coursed team you're a group and we'll set the group up for you, add your locations, and hand you the login — you don't sign each venue up separately.
