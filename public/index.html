Why owners run it

Great service has a rhythm.

A drink before the glass is empty. Courses timed right. Dessert offered while the table's still lingering. Guests don't feel these as upsells — they feel taken care of. Coursed catches the misses live, while the guests are still in the building — a chance to recover the table, not a post-mortem at closeout.

Your standard, made visible

You define what a well-served table looks like at your restaurant. Coursed scores every table against your six-star ladder, tuned per shift — not a generic script.

See the table that's slipping

Seated timers and pace flags surface the tables that have gone quiet or run long — so your manager walks to the one that needs care, not just the loudest.

Fix it at the table, not at closeout

Empty glasses, an overdue course, a dessert no one offered — all still fixable while the guests are in the building. Coursed shows the gap in time to close it.

Your whole team, one standard

Your standard

The six-star ladder.

Six milestones that mark a table being served the way you'd serve it — built from your own menu and rhythm, tuned per shift. A fully-coursed table has gotten your best.

Milestones are per guest and fully configurable per shift. Six of six is a fully-coursed table.

Coaching & reporting

The manager's other screen.

The board on the floor holds the line during service. In the back, a manager screen turns the same live data into coaching — who's dialed in, who needs a word, and how the team is trending week over week. Nothing to enter, nothing to tally; it's the shift as it actually happened.

Two screens, two jobs

A live board on the pass for the team, and a manager view in the back for coaching, history, and lookup. Give the floor a view-only screen — settings and the numbers stay with you.

Every server, ranked fairly

A shift leaderboard by stars per table — with a minimum-table floor, so a slow night doesn't crown someone on two tables. Coaching from real service, not gut feel or the loudest voice.

Reopen any past shift

Pull up last Friday's dinner table by table, with the fired-item timeline for each seating. Walk a server through exactly where a table stalled — days later, from the office, with the record in front of you.

See who's improving

Reports over any range: stars per table trending up or down, which ladder rungs keep getting missed, category attach and net per cover. The numbers that tell you where to coach next — and whether it's working.

Every pace, low-guest, and fast-turn flag a screen dismisses is logged with who cleared it — so you always know what got waved off, and by the floor or a manager.
